# 8.7K views · 400 reactions | This is how to get people addicted to your storytelling even if you talk about super boring topics. It’s called the illusion of novelty. And this one framework is the ultimate storytelling hack for boring businesses. Step one is the new reveal. The first thing you have to do to make a boring topic feel new, is reveal that something has changed about it. Let’s say I make content about drinking water, the most boring topic ever. If I say, drinking water is good for you, well then I’ve lost your attention because you already knew that. But instead if I say, there’s new research that says drinking water at a specific time of day will spike your energy more than coffee, all of a sudden, I just revealed something new about that old topic. Now step two in the framework is called Contrast Framing. And this is when you take that new reveal and position it against the old way of thinking. In the water example, I might say something like this. Now everyone knows you should drink 8 cups of water per day to stay healthy. Well it turns out, that number is actually made up, and what really matters more isn’t how much you drink, but when. See now I’ve created a comparative relationship between what you currently believe, the amount of water, and my new reveal, the timing. And this makes you a lot more curious to see if my new reveal is true. Step three in the framework is called Urgency. And I like to refer to this as the turbo button. If you really want to make your stories 10 out of 10 addicting, you should add urgency on top of your new reveal. And this is usually something based around timeliness, something just happened or something is about to close forever. So for the water example, it could be something like a new study just published that shows the timeliness of drinking water matters. Those four steps combined together to create the illusion that something is novel, even if it’s actually boring.
